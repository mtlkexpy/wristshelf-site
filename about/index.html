About Wristshelf

Wristshelf brings the audiobooks and podcasts on your Audiobookshelf server to Apple Watch, with no iPhone companion app required.

Sign in directly on the watch and browse the libraries your account can access. Your server remains in charge of your collection and account permissions, including a curated library for a child’s account.

Made for listening

  • Start listening right away. Stream from your server, or download ahead for offline listening. Manage downloads with pause, resume and delete controls.
  • Find your place. Skip forward or back, select a chapter, or open the separate position controls when you want to scrub through a title.
  • Listen at your pace. Adjust playback from 0.5× to 3.0× in 0.1 steps, set a sleep timer, and use the Digital Crown for volume on the player.
  • Follow books and podcasts. Switch libraries, browse podcast episodes and filter by listening status.
  • Keep your progress. Listening positions are saved locally and synced back to your server when connected, so you can continue on another device.

What you need

Wristshelf requires Apple Watch with watchOS 27 or later and an account on an Audiobookshelf server reachable from your watch. You enter your server address and credentials on the watch. Wristshelf does not include a media subscription or bundled books and podcasts; ebooks are not supported in this release.

Downloaded audio can play offline. Streaming, new downloads and server synchronization need a connection. Background downloads are scheduled by watchOS, so they may wait for suitable conditions and are not guaranteed to advance immediately after you leave the app.

Wristshelf is preparing for its first public App Store release. It is independent software, not an official Audiobookshelf app. The application code uses the MIT license; third-party media and artwork retain their own rights.

Privacy and support

Your library comes from your own server. See the privacy policy for how credentials, downloads and listening progress are handled. For setup help or a problem, visit Support or email wristshelf_support@fourbees.net.

The gospel

The good news of Jesus Christ is that forgiveness and new life are offered through Him. He died for our sins and rose again. Salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ.

Christ died for our sins and rose again

For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,

1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · BSB (excerpt)

Salvation is a gift of grace

For it is by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God, not by works, so that no one can boast.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · BSB

We cannot earn salvation through our own goodness. God invites us to turn to Him and trust in what Jesus has done.

Believe in Jesus Christ

that if you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. For with your heart you believe and are justified, and with your mouth you confess and are saved.

Romans 10:9–10 · BSB

You can respond by turning to God, acknowledging your need for His forgiveness, and placing your faith in Jesus Christ as Lord. Reading the Gospel of John and speaking with a local Christian church can help you learn more about following Him.
